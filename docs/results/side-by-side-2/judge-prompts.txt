Judges: nine opus agents (xhigh), 2026-11-06, each reading only its own input file and rubric.
Pools: 260 pairs (typed vs A, A vs B; baseline-e2 = ladder-personaplex-1 epoch 2 as decoded by B7, and cons1-e2), 3 judges;
390 single answers (both models, typed/A/B), 4 judges; 208 swap items (turns 2 and 6, typed and A, true and swapped, both models), 2 judges.
Shuffled (seed 11), X/Y and P/Q order random, model, rendering and condition hidden. Swap items do not show the tape tutor answer (it would reveal the dialogue's own turn).

--- pair prompt (rubric, B7 verbatim) ---
For each pair decide, judging SUBSTANCE not wording:
- "same": X and Y make the same pedagogical move on the pupil's turn: the same verdict on what the
  pupil said (agree / correct / ask back), the same mathematical claim or example direction and
  the same next step. Different numbers or wording within the same move still count as "same".
  Small slips are fine.
- "differs": they make a different move: one accepts what the pupil said and the other corrects
  it; they reach different conclusions; one engages the pupil's turn and the other ignores it or
  answers something else; they go to clearly different content.
Judge mainly the first two sentences (the part that responds to the pupil); later drift counts
only if it contradicts the opening.
Also give, for each pair, "x_engages" and "y_engages": "yes" if the answer takes up the specific
thing the pupil said (their answer, claim, number or question), "no" if it does not.
Output keys: id, substance, x_engages, y_engages, why (at most 15 words).

--- answer prompt (rubric, B7 verbatim + well_formed) ---
- "engages": "yes" if its opening takes up the specific thing the pupil said (their answer,
  claim, number, guess or question: e.g. agrees with it, corrects it, quotes or reuses it); "no"
  if it carries on the lesson without reference to what the pupil said, or answers something the
  pupil did not say.
- "verdict": "agree", "correct", "ask_back", "none", or "na" for an opening question by the pupil.
- "matches_tape": "yes" if its move on the pupil's turn is the same as the tape_tutor_answer's
  (same verdict and direction), "no" otherwise.
Judge mainly the first two sentences.
- "well_formed": "no" if the answer is garbled, broken mid-word, ungrammatical to the point of
  nonsense, a bare fragment, or loops repeating itself; "yes" otherwise. A silent answer is "no".
Output keys: id, engages, verdict, matches_tape, well_formed, why (at most 12 words).

--- swap prompt (rubric, new) ---
Each item shows the previous tutor turn, two candidate pupil turns P and Q, and the tutor's
answer. Exactly one of P and Q is what the pupil said right before this answer; the other is a
decoy. Decide which pupil turn the answer's OPENING takes up (agrees with, corrects, quotes or
reuses its specific answer, number, claim or question). Judge only by the answer's content, not
by which pupil turn fits the previous tutor turn better: the pupil may have said something odd.
- "P" or "Q": the opening clearly takes up that one and not the other.
- "both": it is consistent with both equally (e.g. both said the same number).
- "neither": it takes up neither (generic, off-topic, silent, or a number neither said).
Judge mainly the first two sentences.
Output keys: id, takes_up, why (at most 12 words).
